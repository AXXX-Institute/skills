Progressive Cramming: Reliable Token Compression and What It Reveals

Dmitrii Tarasov1,2, Timofei Lashukov2, Elizaveta Goncharova1,2, Andrey Kuznetsov1,3
1 Progressive cramming

Optimise a per-sample input embedding $\mathbf{e}$ so that a frozen LM reconstructs a text. The target grows one token at a time; the next stage starts only after perfect greedy reconstruction of the current prefix.

  • Halting on the first failure gives a sharp capacity boundary per sample.
  • 100% greedy reconstruction at every accepted stage.
  • Saving $\mathbf{e}$ after each stage yields the optimisation trajectory.

Low-dim projection. Optimise $\mathbf{z}$ with a per-sample affine map $W, \mathbf{b}$:

$$\mathbf{e} = W\mathbf{z} + \mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^d, \qquad \mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{R}^k,\; k \ll d$$
3 Optimisation trajectories
  • Trajectories are intrinsically low-dimensional: 30–100 PCA components explain 99% of the variance, although they live in $\mathbb{R}^{4096}$.
  • Accuracy basins shrink with sequence length — longer texts leave less room for error.
Llama-3.1-8B trajectory on PC1–PC2 (65.7% of variance); coloured regions are local accuracy basins.
4 Reconstruction ≠ understanding

Prepending a crammed embedding drops benchmark accuracy even with the original prefix in context:

ModelHS baseHS +embARC baseARC +emb
Pythia-1.4B44.0%37.6%53.1%49.1%
SmolLM2-1.7B53.6%37.0%67.3%56.0%
Llama-3.1-8B61.9%40.8%63.5%43.3%

On generative 5-shot MMLU accuracy falls to ≈0% with the optimised embedding, while a random-embedding control stays at the baseline.

5 Attention knockout
Llama-3.1-8B. Forward knockout (masking early layers first) restores downstream accuracy, while reverse knockout fails until the early layers are masked too.

Early layers drive both the reconstruction and the capability collapse; the 20–60% late-layer attention mass is a symptom. The pattern holds for Llama-3.1-8B, Pythia-1.4B and SmolLM2-1.7B.

6 Why not full cramming?

Full cramming fixes the number of tokens and optimises them jointly; progressive cramming fixes accuracy at 100% and grows the text. Full cramming reaches more tokens, but its residual errors sit at early positions, and one early miss cascades through greedy generation:

7 Takeaways
  • Brittle steering. Perfect reconstruction stores nothing the model can use: benchmarks drop and generative MMLU collapses to ≈0%.
  • Early layers. The embedding's causal influence concentrates in the first layers across all model families.
